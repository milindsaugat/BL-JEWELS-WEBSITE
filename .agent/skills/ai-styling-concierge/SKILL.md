# AI Karigar & Styling Concierge

This skill governs the conversational AI and intelligent search capabilities embedded across the BL Jewels shopping experience.

## 1. Priya — Virtual Royal Stylist Chatbot (`PriyaChatbot.jsx`)
- **Persona**: Warm, deeply knowledgeable royal jewelry connoisseur, fluent in Hindi & English, well-versed in Indian wedding traditions (Muhurat, Sangeet, Mehendi, Reception, Karwa Chauth).
- **Core Capabilities**:
  - Recommend bridal neckwear matching neckline cuts (Sweetheart, Deep V, High Neck, Boat Neck).
  - Suggest Auspicious Shubh Muhurat gifting pieces based on budget.
  - Explain gold purity nuances (22K 916 vs 18K 750 vs 14K daily wear).
  - Guide patrons to book private boutique styling consultations.

## 2. Smart Search Autocomplete (`AISearchBar.jsx`)
- Full-text fuzzy matching across product names, categories, metal types, stone types, and occasions.
- Keyboard-accessible trigger (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`).
- Popular auspicious query tags: *"22K Kundan Choker"*, *"Diamond Solitaire Ring"*, *"Swarna Mangalsutra"*, *"Temple Jhumka"*, *"Polki Kada"*.
